# sparklabory

Create a mobile educational app for students and teachers that facilitates scientific research and school project development. The app should include:

1. **User Accounts and Profiles**: student, teacher, and researcher profiles.
2. **Project Management**: create, track, and document scientific projects, with steps of the scientific method (hypothesis, experiment, results, conclusion).
3. **Research Tools**: integration with academic databases (Google Scholar, SciELO), report templates, automatic reference generation.
4. **Virtual Laboratory**: simulations of physics, chemistry, and biology experiments, with step-by-step instructions and animated results.
5. **Collaboration and Communication**: internal chat, video conferencing, document uploads, group task management.
6. **Gamification and Engagement**: science quizzes, weekly challenges, student ranking, badges or points.
7. **Export and Sharing**: generate PDF or Word reports, share project results with peers and teachers.
8. **User Interface**: friendly, intuitive, colorful, with simple navigation and clear menus for different types of users.
9. **Extras**: suggestions for research topics, deadline alerts, and space to publish local scientific works.

The app should include: home screens, menus, navigation flows, example screens for projects, virtual labs, and collaboration chats.
